01

What this policy covers

This policy explains how VISIONPLACE uses cookies and similar browser storage on visionplace.site. Cookies are small text files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include browser storage, which allows a website to remember limited information on a device.

02

Our approach

We keep browser storage to a minimum. Essential storage is used to remember your cookie choice and support basic interactions. Optional measurement technologies are not activated unless you choose “Accept all” in the cookie notice. The current website does not use advertising cookies or cross-site behavioural profiling.

03

Browser storage

  • visionplace:cookiePreference — remembers whether you selected essential storage only or accepted all categories. It remains until cleared from your browser.
  • visionplace:lastEnquiry — supports the project enquiry confirmation and is automatically cleared by the browser.

These entries support essential website functions and do not enable advertising or cross-site tracking.

04

Essential technologies

Essential technologies are required for functionality, security or to remember a choice you have made. They cannot be switched off through the cookie banner because the site would not be able to remember that you declined optional storage. You can still remove them through your browser settings.

05

Optional analytics

Optional analytics may be introduced to understand aggregate website usage, such as which pages are visited and whether the site functions correctly across device types. These tools will only be enabled after consent where required. If enabled, the cookie notice and this page will be updated with provider names, purposes and retention periods.

06

Managing your choice

You can choose “Essential only” or “Accept all” when the cookie notice appears. You can reset the choice at any time by clearing site data for visionplace.site in your browser. Most browsers also let you block cookies, delete stored data or receive a warning before storage is created. Blocking all storage may affect some interactions.

07

Third-party links

Links to Instagram, Behance, LinkedIn and other external websites open services governed by their own cookie and privacy policies. VISIONPLACE does not control storage created after you leave this website.

08

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the website or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions can be sent to hello@visionplace.site.
